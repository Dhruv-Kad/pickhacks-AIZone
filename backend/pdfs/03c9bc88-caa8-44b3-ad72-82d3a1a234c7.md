# Welcome to Ballwin

Dear Ballwin Resident,


Welcome! We’re so excited to have you join our community. Ballwin is home to
countless businesses, amenities, schools, churches, restaurants, and now... you!


To help you get acquainted with our city and answer some questions you may
have, we’ve put together this welcome guide. This guide gives you a snapshot
of each of our departments and facilities, but it is far from exhaustive. If you have
further questions, please do not hesitate to contact us or visit our website at
www.ballwin.mo.us.


Ballwin has consistently been named one of the best places to live. Ballwin was
named One of America’s Best Places to Live by Money Magazine several times;
one of the top ten Safest Cities in Missouri by the National Council for Home Safety
and Security, Safewise, Homesnacks and Niche; among the top ten Suburbs in the
St. Louis region according to WalletHub; one of the top ten Best Places to Raise
a Family in Missouri by Niche for the past five years; One of the Best Cities to Live
in Missouri by the Chamber of Commerce; one of the Best Places to Live in the
Midwest by Kearney Hub; and ranked in the 86th percentile for Best Small Cities in
America.


Connect with us and let us know how your transition is going and how we can
meet your needs as a valued resident. We hope you enjoy living the #BallwinLife.

# City Services

**Snow Removal**
The Ballwin Public Works Department tries to make winter a bit more manageable
by handling snow removal in the City. Crews treat the streets just before snow
or freezing rain begin. Once a snow emergency occurs, crews work around
the clock to clear the roadways. It’s important to note, not all streets in Ballwin
are maintained by Ballwin crews (Manchester Rd. is maintained by the Missouri
Department of Transportation). To prepare for winter weather, please read Section
15-266 in our Code of Ordinances which covers vehicles on City streets during a
snow emergency.

**Leaf Collection**
Every fall, our Public Works crews begin our curbside Leaf Collection program,
which provides a convenient way for residents to dispose of their leaves. More
information on our Leaf Collection program can be found at www.ballwin.mo.us/
Curbside-Leaf-Collection/.


**Tree Planting Program**
The City of Ballwin is pleased to offer a cost-share program to encourage the
planting of trees in our neighborhoods to provide beauty and environmental
benefits. You may apply to the City for up to a $100 reimbursement after planting
a tree, provided the area and the tree meet the appropriate criteria. More
information can be found at www.ballwin.mo.us/Tree-Planting-Program/.


**Road Repairs**
If you notice potholes, tripping hazards, or other street problems, please report
them to our Public Works Department using the Report an Issue feature on our
website at www.ballwin.mo.us/Report-An-Issue/. You can also use this feature to
report a code violation, street tree issue, or a tip to the police.


**Community Programs & Events**
The Ballwin Parks & Recreation Department hosts hundreds of community events
every year. Some of our fan favorites include Ballwin Days, Easter Egg Hunt, Tons
of Trucks, Dirty Spokes Mountain Bike Race, Daddy Daughter Dance, Conquer
Castlewood, Craft Beer Festival, Family Hayride, Moonlight Howl 5k, and the Turkey
Bowl Golf Scramble. The department also has several programs offered throughout
the year, such as our summer camps, golf leagues, and indoor and outdoor swim
lessons. You can view the calendar of upcoming events at any time by visiting our
website and clicking the Events Tab.


**Shop Ballwin First**
The City of Ballwin has partnered with several businesses in our community to
provide our Shop Ballwin First program. Participating businesses provide a special
discount which is only available to Ballwin residents. All you need is a current
Resident ID to take advantage of these awesome deals. To get a Resident ID,
bring an unpaid gas, water, electric, or sewer bill to The Pointe at Ballwin Commons
($5 for card, $1 for yearly renewal).

# Resident InformationCity Services

**Property & Real Estate Taxes**
The City’s property tax rate for citizens has been rolled back to $0 since 1987,
leaving City services being financed primarily by sales taxes, utility gross receipts
taxes, and recreational revenues. However, a real estate tax is collected by St.
Louis County, the area school districts, and other agencies. For more information
on taxes, contact our Finance Department at (636) 227-9000.


**Trash, Recycling and Yard Waste**
The City of Ballwin does not provide trash collection services. Instead, we contract
with an independent collection service, Republic Services. General trash,
yardwaste, and recycling guidelines can be found on the City’s website at
www.ballwin.mo.us/Trash-Pick-up-and-Recycling/. However, for the most current
and accurate information, you should contact Republic Services directly (see
page 5) with questions or comments regarding their service.


**Utilities**
Residents may set up utilities with any company who provides the respective
service. The City of Ballwin does not control any of the water, electricity, gas, or
sewage services and their inclusion in this guide is meant as a resource, not an
endorsement. Contact information for Ameren Electric, Missouri American Water,
Spire Gas, and Metropolitan Sewer District can be found on page 5.


**Communication**
The City of Ballwin is committed to open, transparent, and timely communication.
We offer a variety of ways for you to obtain information and stay informed about
public meetings, upcoming events, city finances, police department updates,
parks and recreation activities, and more.


**Website** - You can find the most up-to-date information on everything happening
in the City online at www.ballwin.mo.us.
**Ballwin Life Magazine -** A quarterly publication is mailed to every Ballwin household
and Pointe member. Each issue provides a listing of upcoming events and
programs, a message from the Mayor, and other important information. Current
and past issues can be accessed online at www.ballwin.mo.us/Ballwin-LifeMagazine/.
**Email Newsletters** - If you are wanting specific updates about a variety of topics,
we encourage you to sign up for one, or all, or our e-newsletters, accessible on our
webiste by hovering over the Services tab and selecting E-Newsletter Sign Up.
**Social Media** - The City of Ballwin, Ballwin Parks & Recreation, and the Ballwin
Police Department have a presence on various social media platforms, including
Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram. These are used to share information with the
community.
**TextCaster -** The City of Ballwin uses a text-based alert system to notify residents of
City updates or urgent alerts. Sign up for the notices you’d like to receive on our
website at by hovering over the Services tab and selecting TextCaster Sign Up.

every day to ensure that Ballwin is a wonderful place to live,
work, and play. Our Officers, Dispatchers, and Civilian Staff

**Services and Programs**


**Vacation Checks** - Heading out of town for a short or long trip? Are you looking for
a little extra peace of mind while you are gone? Your Ballwin Police Department
can help you out! We offer a Vacation Check service. By completing a simple
form online or in person at the police station, our department will be notified of
your absence. During this time, the officer assigned to the area of your home
will make sure to keep a close watch on your address. During this process, you
can provide us with important information, such as who will have access to your
home while you are away and your contact information (should we need to
contact you about your residence). We do not need to know where you are going
because it will only make us jealous… To view the form visit www.ballwin.mo.us/
vacationcheck or contact us at (636) 227-9636 with any questions.


**Citizens Academy** - Participants in this program will receive instruction on patrol
techniques, traffic enforcement, accident investigation, crime scene processing,
police communications, and more. Continue to check the website and Ballwin
Police Department social media pages for the next start date.


**Caring Partners Program -** Once each day, residents registered for the Caring
Partners Program will receive a telephone call ensuring they are OK. If the call
is not answered, an officer will be dispatched to the house to ensure everything
is okay. Residents can sign up for this program by contacting the Ballwin Police
Department’s Community Affairs Unit at (636) 227-9636.


**Other Information**
Please contact us for questions, reporting, or to acquire more information about
any of the following topics: Neighborhood Watch, Solicitation Permits, Bicycle
Registration, Archery Deer Hunting, Tours, National Night Out, Explorer Program
Post, Safety Tips, and more.

# Code Enforcement

The Code Enforcement Division is responsible for protecting your health and safety,
maintaining your city, neighborhood, and your home, assuring the long-term value
of your investment, and the viability of our community.


**Permits**
Permits are required for many construction projects, including, but not limited to,
tool sheds, fences, decks, and swimming pools. To learn more and obtain these
permits, please visit the Document Center section on our website.


**Housing Inspections/Occupancy Permits**
All commercial properties and dwelling units must be inspected and certified for
compliance before they can be sold or rented. New occupants are required to
obtain an Occupany Permit.


**Property Maintenance**
City code prohibits the accumulation of trash and growth of vegetation over six
inches in height.


**Sewer Lateral Repair Program**
Residential homes in Ballwin are enrolled in a sewer lateral insurance program
available through the City. A sewer lateral is the pipe that carries household waste
to the Metropolitan Sewer District (MSD) sewer main. Information on the Sewer
Lateral Repair Program can be found on the City’s website at www.ballwin.mo.us/
Sewer-Lateral-Repair-Program/.


**Contact Us**
If you have further questions about code enforcement, the Ballwin Housing Code,
permits, or inspections, please contact the Code Enforcement Department at
(636) 227-2129. Ballwin’s complete Code of Ordinances is also available for your
reference at library.municode.com/mo/ballwin/codes/code_of_ordinances.
