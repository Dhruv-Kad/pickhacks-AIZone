PROHIBITED LOCATIONS.
Baniers shall be located so as to prohibit permanent structures, equipment or similar objects from being used
to climb the baniers.


BARRIER EXCEPTIONS.
Spas or hot tubs with a safety cover which complies with ASTM F 1346, as listed in Section 4G107, shall be
exempt from the provisions of this appendix.
